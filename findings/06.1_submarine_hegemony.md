# Finding 6.1 — The SMW5 fault in the control plane: rerouting happened above the duopoly, not below it

**Experiment:** `experiments/06.1_submarine_hegemony/` · **Extends:** Exp 06 (same event, data
plane) · **Data:** daily AS-hegemony (IIJ IHR), 15 Jun – 10 Jul 2026, bracketing the 2 Jul fault.

## Headline

> During the SMW5 submarine-cable fault, **domestic networks' dependencies on the two operators
> stayed frozen** (7 of 8 probe ISPs unchanged; Orbit pinned at TWA = 1.00 throughout), while
> **the operators re-carriered ~21% (TWA) and ~13.5% (PTCL) of their world paths for ~48 hours**
> — both swinging onto Hurricane Electric on exactly 1–2 Jul. Users rode out the fault on
> unchanged routes; the duopoly absorbed it upstream.

Combined with Exp 06 (data plane: congestion and jitter, no transit change), the event is now
characterised in **both planes** — consistent, complementary, from independent measurement
systems.

## The two-leg picture

**Leg 1 — local ISPs → the gates (baseline 26–30 Jun → fault day):** frozen. The one exception:
**Fasttel** swapped its majority from PTCL (0.74 → 0.46) to TWA (0.26 → 0.54) for ~2 days,
reverting by 3 Jul. Its shift began **1 Jul, a day before the PTA announcement** — evidence the
fault was operative before it was reported (Exp 06 inferred the same), *not* of foreknowledge.

**Leg 2 — TWA → the world's carriers (unfiltered data):**

| TWA's carriers | before | **2 Jul** | after |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Cogent | 16% | **4%** ⬇ | 16% |
| Sparkle | 8.5% | 4.7% ⬇ | 7.4% |
| **Hurricane Electric** | 2% | **20.5%** ⬆ | 2.3% |

**Leg 2 — PTCL → the world:** same pattern, smaller magnitudes (PTCL reaches the world mostly by
peering, so its transit dependency is diffuse): **Hurricane 0% → 14.2% on exactly 1–2 Jul → 0%**,
Etisalat 2.1% → 7.4%, Arelion spiking 7.1% on the 2nd; Level3's decline began pre-fault (not
attributed). Both operators side-by-side: `results/fig_operators_upstreams.png`.

Route churn (fraction of world paths changing carrier vs baseline): TWA 2.1% → **20.9%** → 3.5%;
PTCL 11.9% (already elevated 1 Jul) → 13.5% → 3.9%. Normal-day churn is 2–6%.

## Verification & honesty ledger

All operator-level numbers re-verified on an **unfiltered** pull (the first pull dropped values
< 0.02, which can fake "appearances"). One sub-claim was **retracted** in the process (a "new"
PTCL→Cogent link — it pre-existed below the filter). IHR's own archive has gaps on the fault days
(45/96 bins on 2 Jul, 23/96 on 3 Jul) — the large swings dwarf this, but small day-level effects
on 2–3 Jul are not to be over-read. Hegemony counts **paths, not traffic**.

## Physical-cable connection (stated carefully)

Hegemony sees logical (BGP) routes, not fibre. The mechanism linking them: carrier interconnects
ride leased capacity on specific submarine systems; the fault killed the lanes under some
interconnects → their announcements withdrew → the world re-selected paths via surviving
interconnects → the shares moved. The 2 Jul churn spike is the **BGP shadow of the physical
failure** — attribution by timing (3–10× baseline, both operators, same substitute carrier) plus
mechanism, since no public carrier↔cable mapping exists. Obtaining that mapping (asks itemised in
the experiment notes; route: Nowmay/AINTEC'24) upgrades this to verification and enables a
quantified single-point-of-failure map per cable.
