Full reasoning: edits/2026-09-01_eda_docs_gsl_muscat_correction.md and this
conversation. Rule applied throughout: an ASN/company "country" field (from Team
Cymru, ip-api, or an ASN's own registration) tells you who owns or routes an
address, never where it physically sits. Only a descriptive PTR hostname on the
EXACT ip, or a registrant's own explicit country field in the real per-block
registration record, counts as evidence for this table. A neighboring IP's PTR
does not transfer to IPs near it -- these operators number routers globally out
of shared pools, not per physical site.
