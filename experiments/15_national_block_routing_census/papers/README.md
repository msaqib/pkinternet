# papers — Experiment 15 — block-routing census (single round)

PDFs held here are copies; the canonical set is `../../literature/` (see its `README.md` for the full
filename → paper index).

## Held in this folder

- **`2016_selective_scanning.pdf`** — Klick et al., **TASS**, IMC 2016 — the source of the prefix-as-unit and density ideas. The sampling rule inverts theirs: TASS prunes low-density prefixes, a census keeps every one.
- **`subramanian_old.pdf`** — Subramanian, Padmanabhan, Katz — *Geographic Properties of Internet Routing*. The foundational statement that AS-level topology and geography diverge, which is what a detour is.

## Also relevant, in `../../literature/`

- **`p1.pdf`** — Sanchez et al., *Inter-Domain Traffic Estimation for the Outsider* — inferring interdomain behaviour without operator cooperation, the constraint this whole project works under.
- **`Is_it_really_worth_to_peer_at_IXPs_A_comparative_study.pdf`** — Di Bartolomeo et al. — peering value, for the IXP framing.
- **`The Quest for a Resilient Internet Access…pdf`** — Opalinski et al. — measurement in a constrained geopolitical setting.

## Cited but not held locally — fetch before writing

- **Edmundson et al. 2016**, *Characterizing and Avoiding Routing Detours Through Surveillance States* — the definition of a routing detour and how to find it in traceroutes.
- **Gupta et al., PAM 2014**, *Peering at the Internet's Frontier* — the direct regional antecedent: African traffic detouring via Europe for want of local interconnection.
