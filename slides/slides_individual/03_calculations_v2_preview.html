LATENCY RATIO
Physics floor: fastest RTT the distance allows, at the speed of light in fiber
PK / Abroad sites: floor from straight-line probe-to-server distance
CDN sites: floor is the best RTT any of our probes achieved to that site
ratio = RTT / floor
1x = physical limit, k = k times slower
TROMBONING CLASSIFICATION
Scope: Pakistani-hosted sites only; CDN/Abroad expected to exit anyway
Rule: tromboned if any hop belongs to a foreign-registered network
Exceptions: Shaw Communications & Cogent IPs, foreign-registered but confirmed inside Pakistan by RTT
