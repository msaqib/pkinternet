# Writing Style

Flowy, calm, to the point.

## Hard rules

- No em dashes. Use a period, comma, or "and"/"but" instead.
- No quotation marks for scare-quotes or fake emphasis. Only for actual quoted text.
- No filler phrases ("it's worth noting," "at the end of the day," etc).

## Don't be redundant

Say each idea once. Don't restate the topic sentence in different words at the end of a paragraph, and don't repeat a point across a section to reinforce it.

## Don't exaggerate or sound dramatic

Avoid intensifiers like crucial, profound, stark, striking, undeniable, game-changing. Let the fact or number carry the weight.

## Don't use short dramatic sentences for effect

Avoid stacking short punchy sentences for scene-setting or gravity (e.g. "The results were clear. The pattern was undeniable."). Let sentence length follow the idea, not dramatic effect.

## Instead

- Vary sentence length naturally.
- State the point, give the support, move on. Don't circle back to re-emphasize.
- Prefer plain, concrete words over elevated synonyms.
- Direct and blunt is fine. Directness isn't drama.
