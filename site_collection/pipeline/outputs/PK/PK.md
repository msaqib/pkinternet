## Last two hops for the 12 sites whose destination replied

For 12 of the 26 selected sites the destination itself replied on both probes (ICMP), so the last visible hop is the site's own IP, which is registered and geolocated in Pakistan. The previous visible public hop is registered in Pakistan for 11 of the 12 sites on both probes. The exception is zong.com.pk, whose previous hop is US-registered but shows the same delay as the destination (4 ms and 40 ms), so it is most likely a US-registered address used inside Pakistan.

Hop country is the registry country of the hop address. Private addresses and silent hops are skipped, so the previous hop means the previous visible public hop. The other 14 sites never replied over ICMP and are being re-traced over TCP port 443.
